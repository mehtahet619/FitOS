Good questions

A few things
to know.

Clear expectations make a better plan.

Does FitOS work if my gym has different equipment?

Yes. Add the machines and weights you can use to your equipment list. Workout sessions use that list with your selected goals, split, and available time; you can update it when your setup changes.

Does Live Scan know when a machine is occupied?

No. Equipment recognition, when its vision provider is available, identifies equipment in an image. FitOS does not determine whether a person is using a machine or whether a station is free.

Are nearby food and nutrition details verified?

Nearby suggestions require location access and a working places provider. If no nearby listings are returned, FitOS can still create a general meal plan. Menu availability changes, and nutrition values may be estimates; confirm details with the restaurant.

Can I change my training plan later?

Yes. Update your profile, training split, equipment, or session duration as your needs change. Workout selection follows your saved settings and history, and logged sets can help guide progression.

Is FitOS a medical or nutrition service?

No. FitOS provides fitness and general nutrition planning, not diagnosis or treatment. For medical conditions or individualized dietary care, consult a qualified health professional.
