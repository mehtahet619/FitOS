A few things
to know.

Clear expectations make a better plan.

Does FACHS work if my gym has different equipment?

Yes. Add the machines and weights you can use to your equipment list. Workout sessions use that list with your selected goals, split, and available time; you can update it when your setup changes.

Can Live Scan tell whether equipment is occupied and analyze gym crowds?

Yes. Live Scan has a separate Crowd Analysis section powered by FACHS crowd analysis. It estimates the number of people in view, shows the live crowd level, and can flag equipment as possibly occupied when a detected person overlaps a detected machine. This is a visual estimate, not a guaranteed availability check. Equipment recognition and crowd analysis remain separate sections.

Are nearby food and nutrition details verified?

Nearby suggestions require location access and a working places provider. If no nearby listings are returned, FACHS can still create a general meal plan. Menu availability changes, and nutrition values may be estimates; confirm details with the restaurant.

Can I change my training plan later?

Yes. Update your profile, training split, equipment, or session duration as your needs change. Workout selection follows your saved settings and history, and logged sets can help guide progression.

Is FACHS a medical or nutrition service?

No. FACHS provides fitness and general nutrition planning, not diagnosis or treatment. For medical conditions or individualized dietary care, consult a qualified health professional.

01 / TRAINING

Workout plans & progress

WORKOUTS
How does FACHS create my workout plan?

FACHS uses the training details you provide, such as your goal, experience, preferred split, available equipment, training days, and session duration. Keep your profile and equipment list up to date so the plan reflects your current setup.

Can I change my workout split or equipment?

Yes. Open your profile or equipment settings and update the details that have changed. Your available choices may affect the exercises FACHS can recommend.

What happens if I miss a workout?

Workout plans follow daily progression rules. A session left unfinished from a previous day may expire when the plan advances. An expired session should not be counted as a workout completed today. Open your current workout plan to see the session available for the current day.

Why does FACHS say “Please come back tomorrow”?

This message can appear after today's workout has been recorded as complete. If you completed an older session today and the message appears unexpectedly, refresh the workout page. If it continues, report the issue with the date of the workout and the message shown.

How is workout progress calculated?

Progress reflects completed units in your selected training structure. The number and size of those units depend on the split, so different plans can move in different increments.

Can I edit a completed workout?

Workout editing options depend on the controls available in your workout history. Check the session details before leaving the workout screen.

02 / AI TOOLS

Equipment scanning & form checks

EQUIPMENT & AI
How do I scan gym equipment?

Open the equipment scan feature, allow camera access when your browser asks, and capture a clear image with the equipment visible. You can also manage equipment manually where that option is available.

Why was my equipment not detected?

Try better lighting, move closer, keep the equipment unobstructed, and capture one or a few items at a time. If scanning is unavailable, the recognition service may not be configured or temporarily reachable. You can select equipment manually instead.

Does equipment scanning know whether a machine is free?

Equipment recognition and crowd analysis are separate features. Crowd analysis can estimate people and possible equipment overlap in the camera view, but it cannot guarantee that a machine is available.

How do I troubleshoot camera access?

Use a supported browser, check that the site has camera permission, close other apps using the camera, and reload the page. Camera access generally requires a secure HTTPS connection.

How does exercise form feedback work?

When the form-checking feature is available, it uses camera input and pose information to provide exercise guidance. Keep your full movement visible and follow the on-screen setup instructions. Treat automated feedback as guidance, not a medical assessment.

03 / FOOD

Nutrition & food tracking

NUTRITION
How do I track packaged food?

Use the food scanner to look up a barcode or search by product name. Review the serving size and nutrition details before saving. If product information is missing, use manual entry and copy the values from the food label.

Are calorie and protein values always exact?

No. Values depend on the source and serving information. Some menu or food listings may contain estimates or incomplete data. Check the package label or restaurant information when accuracy matters.

Why can't I find nearby food?

Nearby discovery depends on location permission or a manually entered place, as well as an available places/menu provider. Check your location settings and try again. Listings and menus can change.

Can I set dietary preferences and allergies?

You can add dietary preferences and allergy information in your profile. Always review ingredients with the food provider before eating, especially when you have an allergy. Do not rely on an automatically generated listing as an allergy guarantee.

04 / YOUR GYM

Gym discovery & crowd information

GYM DISCOVERY
Why can't FACHS find a nearby gym?

Allow location access or search using a place name. Results depend on third-party map data and coverage, so some gyms may be missing or have incomplete details.

Are gym opening hours guaranteed?

No. Displayed hours depend on available listing data and may not include temporary changes or holiday schedules. Confirm directly with the gym before travelling.

How should I interpret crowd information?

Crowd information is an estimate based on available data or visual analysis, depending on the feature. It is not a live guarantee of occupancy, waiting time, or equipment availability.

05 / YOUR ACCOUNT

Login, privacy & security

ACCOUNT
I didn't receive my email verification or login code. What should I do?

Check that the email address is correct, look in spam or junk folders, and wait for the resend timer before requesting another code. If the code still does not arrive, try again later and report the issue.

How do I reset my password?

Choose “Forgot your password?” on the login screen, request a reset code, and follow the instructions to choose a new password.

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile or settings, choose the edit option, update your details, and save your changes.

Does FACHS provide medical advice?

No. FACHS is a fitness and general nutrition planning tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
