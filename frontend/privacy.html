FITOS · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This page explains the categories of information FitOS uses to provide its features. FitOS is still evolving; this policy describes the current product at a high level and will need updating if its data practices change.

1. Information you provide

When you create and use an account, FitOS may process your name, email address, password hash, account verification information, and profile choices such as fitness goals, training level, available equipment, workout split, session duration, dietary preferences, allergies, and budget. Workout and progress features may process plans, exercise selections, and logged training information that you enter or generate through the service.

2. Camera and image features

If you choose to use camera-based features, your browser requests camera access. Live pose analysis is designed to run in your browser using a pose model. If you submit an image for equipment recognition, that image may be sent to the FitOS backend and the configured vision provider to return a result. Avoid including other people or sensitive material in submitted images. You can stop camera use by stopping the feature or revoking camera permission in your browser.

3. Location and food discovery

Nearby food discovery may use location information when you choose to use that feature and grant browser permission. You can deny permission, but nearby results may then be unavailable. Restaurant/menu data may come from third-party providers and can be incomplete or outdated.

4. Service operation and security

FitOS uses its backend and hosting infrastructure to process requests and store account and product data. Authentication uses browser storage and session cookies as part of the sign-in flow. Basic request, security, and diagnostic logs may be generated to operate the service and investigate errors. Do not share passwords or one-time verification codes with anyone.

5. Third-party providers

Some functions depend on hosting, database, email delivery, AI/vision, and places/menu providers. When a feature needs one of these providers, relevant request data may be processed by that provider to deliver the feature. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply. The exact providers can change as the service develops.

6. Community price guesses

The landing-page price-guess feature accepts a monthly price estimate and updates an average and count in backend process memory. It is not designed to write those guesses to the FitOS application database. The in-memory totals can be reset by a backend restart and may differ across backend instances.

7. Retention and security

FitOS uses security measures appropriate to a developing web application, but no online service can promise absolute security. Retention periods, deletion workflows, and the final operator contact process are not yet published in a sufficiently specific form on this page. Do not submit information you do not want processed by the service.

8. Your choices

You can choose what profile details to provide, decline optional camera or location permissions, and stop using the service. Browser controls can be used to clear locally stored site data and revoke permissions. Clearing local data may sign you out, but it does not necessarily delete information already stored on the backend.

9. Children and sensitive information

FitOS is a general fitness planning tool, not designed to collect medical records. Do not submit sensitive health information or images of other people. If you are not legally able to agree to these terms where you live, do not create an account without the required consent.

10. Policy changes and contact

This policy may be revised when features or data practices change. A dedicated public privacy contact is not currently listed here. Before broad public launch, FitOS should publish an operator identity and contact channel for privacy requests, along with concrete retention and deletion procedures.

This page is transparent about current known product behavior but is not a substitute for a legal review. Confirm the deployed providers, data retention, deletion options, and applicable legal obligations before treating it as a final compliance policy.
