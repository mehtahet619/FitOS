# FitOS

**Train smarter with workouts built around your gym, your goals, and your routine.**

FitOS is a personal fitness companion that helps you plan workouts, understand your progress, make better food choices, and stay consistent.

## Features

### 🏋️ Personalized workouts
- Get workout plans based on your fitness goals, experience, available equipment, and training schedule.
- Follow structured workout splits and daily training sessions.
- Get alternative exercises when your gym does not have the equipment you need.
- Track completed sets, reps, weights, and workout history.
- Receive progressive workout suggestions based on your training history.

### 📸 Smart gym equipment scanning
- Scan the equipment around you using your camera.
- Identify supported gym equipment and use it to shape your workout.
- Get exercise suggestions based on the equipment available to you.

### 🎯 Exercise form and rep tracking
- Use your camera during supported exercises.
- Track repetitions and receive live form guidance.
- Get movement feedback for supported exercises such as squats, curls, and push-ups.

### 🥗 Personalized nutrition
- Get daily calorie and nutrition targets based on your profile and goals.
- Explore meal suggestions that consider your dietary preferences, allergies, and budget.
- Keep your nutrition plan aligned with your fitness routine.

### 🍽️ Food discovery
- Explore food places around you.
- Find nearby options and review available food information.
- Discover healthier choices when verified dish information is available.

### 📍 Gym discovery and attendance
- Find gyms around a selected location.
- Check in to your gym manually or by scanning a QR code.
- Review your gym attendance history.

### 📊 Progress and motivation
- View your training activity and progress on your dashboard.
- Build consistency with monthly goals and workout milestones.
- Earn rewards and track your progress with friends.
- Follow the Grow Your Impact journey as you stay active.

### 🌗 A comfortable experience
- Switch between light and dark themes.
- Drag the theme switch anywhere on the screen and keep it where you prefer.
- Use FitOS on desktop or mobile, including installing it on supported devices.

## Built around your routine

FitOS brings workout planning, exercise guidance, nutrition, gym activity, and progress tracking together in one place.

**Your gym. Your goals. Your pace. Your FitOS.**
