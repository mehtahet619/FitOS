# FACHS

**Your personal fitness companion for smarter workouts, better food choices, and consistent progress.**

FACHS brings workout planning, gym insights, nutrition tracking, and fitness progress together in one place. It helps you make informed decisions based on your goals, your available equipment, and your daily activity.

## What FACHS does

### Personalized workouts
- Creates workout plans around your fitness goal, training split, available gym days, and session time.
- Recommends exercises that match the equipment available to you.
- Suggests alternative exercises when a machine or piece of equipment is unavailable.
- Helps you follow your workout plan and keep track of completed exercises and sets.
- Supports progressive training by using your workout history to guide future sessions.

### Gym and equipment scanning
- Scans gym equipment and identifies supported machines and equipment.
- Suggests exercises that can be performed with the equipment found in your gym.
- Provides a separate crowd-analysis experience to help you understand how busy the gym appears.
- Keeps equipment recognition and crowd analysis as distinct tools within the Gym Scan experience.

### Food and nutrition
- Tracks the food you eat and your daily calorie and macronutrient intake.
- Lets you look up food, scan packaged-food barcodes, and enter nutrition details manually.
- Explains how a serving fits your daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat, and fibre intake.
- Adapts nutrition guidance to your selected goal, including weight loss, weight gain, and body recomposition.
- Suggests what you could eat next to help balance your daily intake.
- Gives nutrition scores and health information as guidance, not restrictions. You remain free to choose and log any food.

### Food discovery
- Helps you explore food options around you.
- Shows available food information and nutrition details where provided.
- Highlights options that may better support your nutrition goals without hiding other choices.

### Daily progress
- Brings your daily fitness activity and progress into one dashboard.
- Helps you stay aware of your workout consistency and monthly activity.
- Tracks completed workouts and progress towards your activity goals.

### Gym check-in
- Lets you mark your gym attendance.
- Supports QR-based and manual check-in.
- Helps prevent duplicate check-ins on the same day.

### Grow Your Impact
- Turns workout consistency into progress through a virtual growth journey.
- Shows growth stages based on qualifying completed workouts.
- Lets you choose a virtual tree as a representation of your commitment.
- Clearly distinguishes virtual progress from real-world planting.

## Built around your choices

FACHS is designed to guide, not control. Your workout recommendations can adapt when equipment is unavailable, and nutrition insights help you understand your choices without restricting them. You stay in control of your training, food, and fitness journey.
